# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Commercial Follow-Up Engine v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence evaluates commercial progress and recommends the most appropriate governed follow-up action.

The objective is to preserve commercial momentum, identify risk, advance qualified opportunities, and avoid repetitive or context-free follow-up.

The model supports both Business Accounts and Individual Prospects.


---


# Commercial Context


The engine evaluates:


- prospect context;
- Business Account or Individual Prospect;
- persona context;
- decision role when applicable;
- commercial intent;
- interaction history;
- Discovery readiness;
- opportunity readiness;
- previous commercial actions.

# Commercial Milestone Tracking


The engine identifies the current and pending commercial milestone.


Possible milestones include:


- obtain additional context;
- confirm business or personal need;
- identify relevant Aura capability;
- identify decision role;
- complete Executive Discovery;
- qualify commercial opportunity;
- schedule commercial conversation;
- deliver proposal;
- review proposal;
- negotiate;
- obtain commercial decision.


---


# Momentum Analysis


Commercial momentum is evaluated using:


- recent engagement;
- response frequency;
- meeting activity;
- progression between milestones;
- content interaction;
- explicit interest;
- timing signals.


Momentum classifications:


POSITIVE MOMENTUM


STABLE MOMENTUM


DECLINING MOMENTUM


NO RECENT MOMENTUM


---


# Risk Analysis


Commercial risk may include:


- prolonged inactivity;
- repeated unanswered outreach;
- unclear need;
- missing decision context;
- low intent;
- stalled Discovery;
- stalled proposal;
- timing mismatch;
- disengagement.


Risk classifications:


LOW RISK


MEDIUM RISK


HIGH RISK


STALLED


---


# Follow-Up Recommendation


Aura Growth Intelligence recommends context-aware follow-up.


Possible recommendations include:


- continue nurturing;
- send relevant content;
- request additional context;
- invite to Executive Discovery;
- schedule commercial conversation;
- follow up after Discovery;
- follow up on proposal;
- contact relevant decision role;
- escalate active opportunity;
- wait for additional intent signals;
- place opportunity on hold;
- recommend closure as lost.


---


# Follow-Up Timing


The engine may recommend:


- immediate follow-up;
- scheduled follow-up;
- event-triggered follow-up;
- no follow-up until a new signal appears.


Timing must consider:


- prospect preference;
- previous interactions;
- commercial urgency;
- momentum;
- risk;
- communication frequency.


---


# Commercial Escalation


Escalation may be recommended when:


- intent is high;
- Discovery is complete;
- opportunity readiness is confirmed;
- decision-maker involvement is required;
- proposal or negotiation requires executive intervention;
- commercial risk requires attention.


Escalation does not grant autonomous authority.

# Intelligent Follow-Up Principle


Aura Growth Intelligence must avoid blind repetitive follow-up.


The engine should answer:


Why should we follow up?


What commercial objective should advance?


Who should be contacted?


Which message or asset is relevant?


When should the action occur?


What evidence supports the recommendation?
