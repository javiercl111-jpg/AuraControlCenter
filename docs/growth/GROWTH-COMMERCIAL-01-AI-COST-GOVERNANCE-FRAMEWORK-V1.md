# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# AI Cost Governance Framework v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This framework defines the economic governance rules for AI capabilities inside Aura Growth Intelligence.

The objective is to control AI operational costs while maintaining intelligence quality, scalability, and enterprise value.


---

# Governance Principles


## Cost Awareness

Every AI capability must consider:

- complexity;
- required intelligence level;
- expected operational cost.


## Usage Governance

Aura must control:

- tenant consumption;
- module consumption;
- request volume;
- operational limits.


## Cost-Based Routing

AI routing decisions must consider:

- quality;
- cost;
- privacy;
- complexity.


## SaaS Cost Control

Aura must support visibility into:

- cost per tenant;
- cost per capability;
- cost per interaction;
- operational margin.


---

# Cost Levels


## C1 — LOW COST

Purpose:

Simple intelligence operations.


Examples:

- classification;
- basic responses;
- simple tasks.


## C2 — STANDARD

Purpose:

Business intelligence operations.


Examples:

- commercial analysis;
- recommendations;
- structured generation.


## C3 — PREMIUM

Purpose:

Advanced intelligence operations.


Examples:

- executive strategy;
- complex reasoning;
- deep analysis.

# Governance Rules


1. Cost governance must occur before AI execution.

2. Routing decisions must optimize value and cost.

3. Tenant consumption must remain measurable.

4. AI scaling must preserve SaaS sustainability.


---

# Future Evolution


Future phases may include:

- AI usage metering;
- cost dashboards;
- tenant quotas;
- provider cost optimization.
