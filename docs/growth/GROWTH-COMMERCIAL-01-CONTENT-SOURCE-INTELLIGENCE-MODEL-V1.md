# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Content Source Intelligence Model v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines the governed content sources available for Aura Growth Intelligence.

The objective is to enable controlled creation, reuse, and transformation of commercial content assets.


---

# Content Sources


## Aura Content Library


Official Aura managed assets.


Includes:

- brand assets;
- product assets;
- video assets;
- commercial materials;
- case studies.


---


## User Provided Content


Content uploaded by authorized users.


Examples:

- videos;
- images;
- presentations;
- recordings;
- documents.


Lifecycle:


Upload Asset

        |

        v


Content Analysis

        |

        v


Metadata Extraction

        |

        v


Classification

        |

        v


Available Content Asset


---


## AI Generated Assets


Assets created using AI capabilities.


Examples:

- images;
- scripts;
- videos;
- commercial copy.


Governance:


AI Generate

        |

        v


Human Review

        |

        v


Approval

        |

        v


Publication


---


## External Authorized Sources


External content sources must provide:


- identified origin;
- authorized usage;
- traceability;
- compliance validation.


---


# Asset Classification


Each content asset must preserve:


- asset type;
- origin;
- owner;
- authorization;
- creation date;
- permitted usage.
