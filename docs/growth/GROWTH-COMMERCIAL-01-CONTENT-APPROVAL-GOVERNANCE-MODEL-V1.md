# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Content Approval Governance Model v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines the governance process required before AI-generated content becomes available for commercial publication.

The objective is to maintain human control, quality, compliance, and traceability.


---

        v

        v

        v

        v

# Review Queue


Content states:


DRAFT


        |


UNDER_REVIEW


        |


APPROVED


        |


REJECTED


        |


PUBLISHED


---


# Human Reviewer


The reviewer validates:


- message quality;
- brand alignment;
- accuracy;
- tone;
- compliance;
- asset usage.


---


# Approval Decision


Available actions:


APPROVE


REJECT


REQUEST_CHANGES


---


# Audit Trail


Every approval decision preserves:


- content_id;
- reviewer;
- decision;
- timestamp;
- comments;
- version.
