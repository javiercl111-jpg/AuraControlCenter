# AI Provider Evaluation Framework v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This framework defines the evaluation methodology for selecting future AI providers for Aura Growth Intelligence.

The objective is to maintain vendor independence while selecting providers according to enterprise capabilities, governance, cost, privacy, and integration compatibility.


---

# Evaluation Principles


## Vendor Independence

Aura does not depend on a single AI provider.

External providers are treated as interchangeable capabilities.


## Capability Based Selection

Provider selection must be based on business capability requirements.


Examples:

- Executive reasoning.
- Enterprise privacy.
- Large scale SaaS optimization.
- Multimodal intelligence.


---

# Evaluation Criteria


## Intelligence Capability

Evaluation:

- reasoning quality;
- business analysis;
- context handling;
- strategic generation.


## Cost Efficiency

Evaluation:

- operational cost;
- scalability;
- cost predictability.


## Privacy & Security

Evaluation:

- data protection;
- isolation;
- enterprise controls.


## Enterprise Readiness

Evaluation:

- SLA;
- support;
- compliance;
- maturity.


## Technical Integration

Evaluation:

- APIs;
- SDKs;
- latency;
- observability.


## Aura Compatibility

Evaluation:

- AI Provider Port compatibility;
- adapter compatibility;
- routing compatibility;
- auditability.

# Governance Rules


1. No provider becomes a core dependency.

2. Provider selection must support future replacement.

3. Sensitive enterprise data requires explicit governance.

4. Routing decisions must remain controlled by Aura Intelligence.
