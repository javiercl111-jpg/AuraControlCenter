# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Intelligent Distribution and Asset Orchestration v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence discovers, governs, selects, adapts, and prepares approved content assets for intelligent commercial distribution.

The objective is to move beyond traditional social scheduling and create governed, context-aware asset orchestration aligned with audience, persona, channel, campaign, and commercial objective.


---


# Asset Source Registry


Aura Growth Intelligence may use approved assets from:


## Aura Content Library

Official Aura-managed assets.


Examples:

- brand assets;
- product images;
- product videos;
- presentations;
- brochures;
- case studies;
- approved campaign materials.


---


## User-Provided Assets

Assets uploaded by authorized users.


Examples:

- videos;
- images;
- recordings;
- presentations;
- documents;
- audio.


---


## Approved AI-Generated Assets

Assets created through approved AI capabilities.


Examples:

- generated images;
- generated video assets;
- generated scripts;
- generated commercial copy;
- generated campaign variants.


Generated assets require traceability and approval before publication.


---


## Authorized External Sources

External assets may be used only when origin and permitted usage are known.


Required evidence includes:

- source;
- owner;
- usage authorization;
- permitted purpose;
- applicable restrictions.

# Rights and Usage Governance


Every asset must preserve:


- asset identifier;
- origin;
- owner;
- tenant;
- usage authorization;
- permitted channels;
- permitted campaign use;
- expiration when applicable;
- version;
- approval status.


An asset without sufficient usage authority must not be published.

# Asset Transformation Recommendations


Aura Growth Intelligence may recommend:


- use asset as-is;
- crop or resize;
- extract video clip;
- combine approved assets;
- add title;
- add subtitles;
- add CTA;
- generate companion copy;
- create channel-specific variant;
- create campaign-specific variant.


Transformation must preserve source traceability.


---


# Video Asset Orchestration


For approved video assets, the engine may recommend:


- scene analysis;
- key moment identification;
- clip extraction;
- sequence selection;
- subtitle generation;
- title generation;
- CTA generation;
- short-form adaptation;
- channel-specific version;
- campaign-specific version.


Multiple approved videos may be evaluated as source material for a new governed content composition.


Actual rendering or editing execution requires an authorized multimedia processing capability.

# Intelligent Distribution Principle


Aura Growth Intelligence must not publish content merely because content is available.


The engine should answer:


Why should this asset be used?


Why is it appropriate for this audience?


Why is this channel appropriate?


Which variant should be used?


When should it be published?


What authorization permits publication?


What evidence will be preserved after execution?
