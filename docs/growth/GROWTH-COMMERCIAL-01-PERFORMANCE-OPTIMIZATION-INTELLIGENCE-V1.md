# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Performance and Growth Optimization Intelligence v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence evaluates outcomes, attributes commercial impact, learns from performance, and recommends future growth decisions.

The objective is to create a closed-loop commercial intelligence system that optimizes campaigns, content, audiences, channels, follow-up, and commercial actions based on evidence.


---


# Outcome Evidence


Aura Growth Intelligence evaluates evidence generated by:


- campaigns;
- content;
- publications;
- emails;
- Executive Discovery;
- commercial conversations;
- opportunities;
- proposals;
- negotiations;
- won outcomes;
- lost outcomes;
- individual prospect conversions;
- business account conversions.


Each outcome should preserve traceable evidence when available.


---

Engagement evidence may include:

# Cost and Efficiency Intelligence


Where cost evidence is available, the system may evaluate:


- content production cost;
- distribution cost;
- campaign cost;
- cost per qualified interaction;
- cost per Discovery;
- cost per opportunity;
- cost per conversion;
- commercial effort;

# Experimentation Intelligence


Aura Growth Intelligence may recommend controlled experiments.


Examples:


- message variant;
- CTA variant;
- asset variant;
- audience variant;
- persona variant;
- channel variant;
- publication timing variant;
- nurturing sequence variant.


Experiments must preserve:


- hypothesis;
- variable;
- comparison group when applicable;
- measurement window;
- outcome evidence;
- result;
- learning.


---


# Pattern Learning


The system may learn from patterns involving:

- successful assets;

- follow-up effectiveness;

# Growth Optimization Principle


Aura Growth Intelligence must optimize for meaningful commercial outcomes, not vanity metrics alone.


The engine should answer:


What actually improved?


What generated qualified commercial movement?


Which audience or persona responded?


Which content or channel contributed?


What did not work?


What should change next?


What evidence supports the recommendation?
