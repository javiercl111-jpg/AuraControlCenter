# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Commercial Intelligence Extension v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model extends Aura Growth Intelligence from lead nurturing into governed commercial intelligence.

The objective is to connect audience, persona, decision role, commercial intent, Discovery readiness, opportunity readiness, and next best commercial action.

The model supports both business prospects and individual prospects.


---


# Prospect Context


Aura Growth Intelligence must distinguish between:


## Business Account

Represents an organization with one or more people involved in a commercial decision.


Business context may include:


- company;
- industry;
- company size;
- business need;
- relevant Aura product;
- buying committee.


---


## Individual Prospect

Represents a person who may independently evaluate, purchase, use, recommend, or influence an Aura capability.


Individual context may include:


- profession;
- interests;
- needs;
- objectives;
- personal intent;
- relevant Aura product.


An individual prospect does not require a Business Account.


---


# Decision Role Intelligence


Where applicable, Aura evaluates the person's commercial role.


Decision roles include:


- Economic Buyer;
- Decision Maker;
- Influencer;
- Champion;
- User;
- Unknown.


Decision Role Intelligence must not assume that every prospect belongs to a buying committee.


---


# Commercial Intent


Commercial intent is evaluated using:


- engagement signals;
- expressed needs;
- product relevance;
- interaction history;
- persona context;
- decision role;
- timing signals.


Intent classifications include:


LOW INTENT


MEDIUM INTENT


HIGH INTENT


READY FOR DISCOVERY


READY FOR OPPORTUNITY


---


# Discovery Readiness


Discovery readiness determines whether a prospect has enough context and intent for Executive Discovery.


Evaluation factors include:


- identifiable need;
- relevant Aura capability;
- sufficient engagement;
- decision context;
- willingness to continue the conversation.


Possible outcomes:


NOT READY


NURTURE


READY FOR DISCOVERY


---


# Opportunity Readiness


Opportunity readiness evaluates whether commercial evidence supports creation or escalation of an opportunity.


Evaluation factors include:


- qualified need;
- relevant solution;
- commercial intent;
- decision role;
- timing;
- next commercial milestone.


Possible outcomes:


NOT QUALIFIED


QUALIFY FURTHER


READY FOR OPPORTUNITY


---


# Next Best Commercial Action


Aura Growth Intelligence recommends the next governed commercial action.


Possible recommendations include:


- continue nurturing;
- send relevant content;
- request additional context;
- invite to Executive Discovery;
- schedule commercial conversation;
- create commercial opportunity;
- escalate to authorized executive;
- wait for additional intent signals.
