# Campaign Intelligence Engine v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence transforms commercial objectives into intelligent campaign strategies and executable actions.

The objective is to create data-driven campaigns aligned with audience, persona, content, and business goals.


---

# Campaign Definition


Every campaign must define:


- campaign name;
- objective;
- audience;
- persona;
- product;
- duration.


---


# Campaign Strategy


Campaign strategy defines:


- message;
- content plan;
- channel plan;
- frequency.


---


# Campaign Actions


Campaign actions include:


- publications;
- emails;
- follow-up;
- events;
- demo invitations.


---


# Campaign Intelligence


The engine provides:


- next best action;
- optimization recommendations;
- strategy adjustments.
