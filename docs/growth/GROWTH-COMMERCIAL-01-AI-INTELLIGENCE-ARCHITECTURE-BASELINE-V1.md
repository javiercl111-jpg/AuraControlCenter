## Recommendation Layer

# Architectural Decisions


1. Aura Growth does not depend on a single AI provider.

2. External AI providers are treated as interchangeable capabilities.

3. Provider adapters isolate vendor-specific implementations.

4. Routing policy enables future cost, complexity, and privacy governance.

5. Execution authority remains outside recommendation layers.
