# GROWTH-COMMERCIAL-01

# Persona Audience Matching Intelligence Model v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence identifies personas and recommends personalized commercial messages.

The objective is to create relevant communication based on audience role, needs, objectives, and intent.


---

# Business Persona Model


## Economic Buyer


Responsible for business impact.


Examples:

- CEO;
- Director General;
- CFO.


Focus:

- ROI;
- growth;
- profitability;
- transformation.


---


## Decision Maker


Responsible for solution selection.


Examples:

- HR Director;
- IT Director;
- Operations Director.


Focus:

- efficiency;
- compliance;
- automation;
- results.


---


## Influencer


Influences purchasing decisions.


Examples:

- HR Manager;
- internal consultant;
- operational leader.


Focus:

- functionality;
- usability;
- experience.


---


## User


Final solution user.


Focus:

- simplicity;
- productivity;
- daily experience.


---

# Individual Persona Model


Represents individual users and professionals.


Evaluation factors:

- profession;
- needs;
- objectives;
- interests;
- personal intent.


---

# Persona Profile


Each persona must preserve:


- role;
- need;
- objective;
- interest;
- pain points;
- preferred message.
