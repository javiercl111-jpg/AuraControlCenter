# Audience and Social Channel Intelligence Model v1.0


## Status

ACTIVE


## Purpose

This model defines how Aura Growth Intelligence identifies audiences and recommends the most effective content distribution channels.

The objective is to support both business audiences and individual audiences.


---

# Audience Classification


## Business Audience


Represents organizations and professional decision makers.


Evaluation factors:

- industry profile;
- company size;
- role;
- decision maker;
- buying committee.


Examples:

- CEO;
- HR Director;
- Operations Manager;
- Technology Leader.


---


## Individual Audience


Represents people and professional users.


Evaluation factors:

- profession;
- interests;
- needs;
- objectives;
- personal intent.


Examples:

- consultant;
- entrepreneur;
- professional user;
- independent specialist.
